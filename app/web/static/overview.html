01 — The Problem

Finding the best deal in Indian quick-commerce means opening four or five apps and doing the comparison yourself.

Swiggy, Zomato, Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, BigBasket, Flipkart Minutes and Amazon Fresh each quote a different headline price. The number that actually matters — item, tax, delivery fee, surge, minus whatever membership discount you hold — is buried a screen or two deeper on every one of them, and it changes by the minute. Shoppers either overpay by skipping the comparison, or spend real time doing it by hand.

02 — The Product

QuickCommerce India asks once, in plain language, and compares all eight apps at the same time — on true all-in price, ETA, rating, and the offers you're actually eligible for.

True all-in price

Item + tax + delivery fee + surge − membership discount, computed the same way across every platform, so the number you see is the number you'd actually pay.

EvidenceDecimal-precision pricing; aware of Swiggy One, Zomato Gold, BB Star and Prime.

Location intelligence

Detect by GPS, type any Indian pincode, or search a city by name — each resolves to a real coverage tier, not a guess.

Evidence72 cities spanning every state & UT; pincode matching keyed to India Post's real numbering structure.

Restaurants that are actually nearby

Food results name the specific restaurant, filtered to a genuine radius of the shopper's location — not just a delivery app's logo.

EvidenceGreat-circle distance computed server-side, capped at 10km; abstains rather than naming somewhere too far.

Search that means it

Typing a specific dish returns that dish — not everything that happens to share one word with it.

EvidenceFull-phrase matching before any fallback; a generic word like "chicken" still browses the whole category.

Explainable by design

Every recommendation states why, cites its source, and grades its own confidence — and says so plainly when it isn't sure.

EvidenceBECAUSE / EVIDENCE / CONFIDENCE on every pick; abstains below a set confidence threshold instead of guessing.

Nothing orders itself

The assistant compares and recommends. A human always approves the actual purchase.

EvidenceIdempotency-keyed approval gate — a retry can't create a duplicate order.
03 — Where This Stands
i

A working MVP, honestly labeled

The comparison engine, location intelligence, and explainability layer are fully functional and live at quickcommerce-india.onrender.com today. The marketplace data behind them — prices, ETAs, ratings, restaurant pairings — is simulated and clearly marked as such in the product, because none of the eight platforms publish a public pricing API to connect to yet.

What's proven is the harder part: the comparison logic, the confidence scoring, and the discipline to abstain rather than invent a number. That's the layer a real data partnership would sit on top of, largely unchanged.
